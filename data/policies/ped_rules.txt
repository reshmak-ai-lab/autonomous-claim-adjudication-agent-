PRE-EXISTING DISEASE RULES
========================================

PED Waiting Period:
36 months.

A condition that existed before policy inception may qualify as
a pre-existing disease depending on the policy definition.

The presence of a PED does not automatically mean the current
claim must be rejected.

The adjudication system must determine:

1. Whether the condition existed before policy inception.
2. Whether it was disclosed.
3. Whether it falls within the waiting period.
4. Whether the condition is medically related to the current claim.
5. Whether the policy contains a specific exclusion.
6. Whether additional medical evidence is required.

Example:

Policy age = 14 months
PED waiting period = 36 months

If a relevant PED existed before policy inception, the PED waiting
period has not yet completed.

However, the system must establish medical relevance before
applying a PED-based adverse decision.

If evidence is insufficient:
Decision = HUMAN_REVIEW
