CLAIM ADJUDICATION RULES
========================================

APPROVE
-------
Use APPROVE when:

- Policy is active.
- Treatment is covered.
- Medical necessity is supported.
- Required documents are available.
- No applicable exclusion is identified.
- No material contradiction exists.
- Fraud risk is low.
- Financial calculations are within policy limits.

PARTIAL_APPROVAL
----------------
Use PARTIAL_APPROVAL when:

- The primary treatment is covered.
- Some expenses are excluded or restricted.
- Room-rent limits cause deductions.
- Non-medical expenses are present.
- Deductibles or co-payment reduce the payable amount.

REJECT
------
Use REJECT only when:

- The treatment is clearly excluded.
- The policy is inactive.
- The claim clearly falls within an applicable exclusion.
- Required coverage conditions are not satisfied.

HUMAN_REVIEW
------------
Use HUMAN_REVIEW when:

- Medical documents contradict one another.
- PED applicability is uncertain.
- Fraud risk is high.
- Duplicate billing is suspected.
- Medical necessity is uncertain.
- Material financial anomalies exist.
- Required evidence is incomplete.
- Model confidence is below the configured threshold.

IMPORTANT:
A fraud signal alone must not automatically result in rejection.

All adverse decisions must be supported by evidence.
