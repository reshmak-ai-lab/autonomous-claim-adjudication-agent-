ROOM RENT RULES
========================================

Room rent limit:
1% of Sum Insured per day.

Example:

Sum Insured = ₹5,00,000

Maximum Room Rent:
₹5,00,000 × 1% = ₹5,000/day

If the insured selects a room costing more than the applicable
room-rent limit, the claim engine must identify the excess amount.

Example:

Actual Room Rent = ₹8,000/day
Eligible Room Rent = ₹5,000/day
Excess = ₹3,000/day

For 3 days:

Actual = ₹24,000
Eligible = ₹15,000
Excess = ₹9,000

Where applicable, proportionate deductions on associated charges
must be calculated according to the policy terms.

The LLM must not independently calculate the final payable amount.
The deterministic claim calculation engine must perform the calculation.
