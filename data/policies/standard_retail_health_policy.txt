STANDARD RETAIL HEALTH POLICY
========================================

Policy Name:
Standard Retail Health Policy

Policy Type:
Individual Retail Health Insurance

Policy Status:
Active

Base Sum Insured:
₹5,00,000

Room Rent Limit:
1% of Sum Insured per day

Maximum Room Rent:
₹5,000 per day

Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period:
36 months

Co-Payment:
10% where applicable

Network Hospital:
Covered subject to policy terms

Cashless Treatment:
Available at approved network hospitals.

Hospitalization:
Hospitalization expenses are payable when medically necessary
and covered under the policy.

Pre-Authorization:
Cashless hospitalization requires pre-authorization where applicable.

Important:
All claims are subject to policy exclusions, waiting periods,
deductibles, co-payment, room-rent restrictions, non-payable items,
and applicable policy terms.

The final claim decision must be based on verified evidence.
