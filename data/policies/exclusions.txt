STANDARD POLICY EXCLUSIONS
========================================

The following categories are generally not payable unless
specifically covered by the applicable policy schedule:

1. Non-medical consumables.
2. Personal comfort items.
3. Administrative charges not directly related to treatment.
4. Registration charges.
5. Visitor charges.
6. Telephone charges.
7. Food charges for attendants.
8. Cosmetic treatment unless medically necessary and covered.
9. Treatment specifically excluded by the policy.
10. Expenses falling entirely within an applicable waiting period.
11. Expenses unrelated to the diagnosed medical condition.

Important:
An excluded line item should not automatically result in rejection
of the entire claim.

The adjudication engine must calculate eligible and non-eligible
components separately.
